General information only — not financial advice. This model simulates differential cash flows and wealth accumulation under Australian tax and lending settings. The mortgage interest rate is held constant across the horizon (rate resets and refinancing are not modeled). Dividend/distribution tax incorporates Australian company tax franking credits (with refundable tax offsets for investors in lower marginal brackets). Capital gains tax on portfolios applies CPI indexation under FY27 rules or the 50% discount under legacy rules. The buyer's principal place of residence (PPOR) is treated as fully CGT exempt. Stamp duty bands and First Home Buyer concessions reflect current state revenue office schedules (updated 2025/2026). Consider seeking advice from a licensed financial adviser before making property or investment decisions.
